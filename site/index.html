How it works

  1. 01

    Share a folder at work

    A small agent runs on your work computer and shares only the folders you pick. Read‑only.

  2. 02

    Ask from home

    The app on your home PC lists those folders and asks for the file you need.

  3. 03

    It flies straight to you

    The two connect directly, encrypted end to end. If a firewall blocks that, a relay forwards bytes it cannot read.

Principles

  • Your files stay on your machine.

    No upload, no sync, no cloud copy. Only the file you fetch lands at home.

  • Encrypted end to end.

    Devices pair once and prove who they are with keys. No ports to open, no VPN to set up at work.

  • Built in public.

    Every day ships a small, working slice, with what broke and what's next.
