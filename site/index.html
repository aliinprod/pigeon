How it works

  1. 01

    Share a folder at work

    A small agent runs on your work computer and shares only the folders you pick. Read‑only.

  2. 02

    Ask from home

    The app on your home PC lists those folders and asks for the file you need.

  3. 03

    It flies straight to you

    The two connect directly, encrypted end to end. If a firewall blocks that, a relay forwards bytes it cannot read.

Principles

  • Your files stay on your machine.

    No upload, no sync, no cloud copy. Only the file you fetch lands at home.

  • Encrypted end to end.

    Devices pair once and prove who they are with keys. No ports to open, no VPN to set up at work.

  • Built in public.

    Every day ships a small, working slice, with what broke and what's next.

FAQ

Is it only computer to computer?

Today, yes: from your work computer to your home computer. Asking for a file from your phone is planned, first through a Telegram bot, later a phone app.

Can my files be in any folder?

You choose which folders to share, and everything inside them is available. Pigeon never opens your whole disk.

What does the agent on my work computer do?

It's a small background app that answers only your paired devices, checks that each requested file is inside a shared folder, and sends it encrypted. It never changes your files. Our tests try more than 20 tricks, like ../, to reach files outside the shared folder, and every change must refuse all of them before it can be merged.

Do my files go to a cloud?

No. Your files stay on your work computer, and only the file you ask for travels to you, encrypted. If a direct connection isn't possible, it passes through a relay server that sees only encrypted bytes. In a real test between a Mac and a Windows PC on two different networks, 10 MB arrived directly in under a second, and through the relay it also arrived intact, just slower.

Can someone steal my files?

Files travel encrypted (TLS 1.3), so anyone in between, the relay included, sees only scrambled data. Only devices you paired, each with its own key, can ask for files, and our tests try wrong keys and oversized messages from strangers on every change. Keep your paired devices locked; you'll be able to remove a lost device from your work computer.

How do you know it's secure?

Every change runs about 40 automated tests on Mac and Windows before it can be merged, including break-in attempts and tests that throw thousands of random inputs at the parsers. Every file is checked with a SHA-256 fingerprint on arrival; if a single byte differs, the file is discarded. A threat model with 28 security requirements guides the work, and every change is scanned for vulnerable dependencies and leaked secrets. Pigeon is still early and has not been independently audited yet.

Does my work computer need to be on?

Yes, for now it has to be on and online. Later we'd like to send the files you worked on home ahead of time.

Is it free?

Pigeon is in early development. It will be free to try when it launches; pricing isn't decided yet.
