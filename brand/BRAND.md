# Pigeon brand

- **Logo:** `logo.webp`, geometric black pigeon in flight on white. Use black on light backgrounds, invert to white on dark. Keep clear space of half the logo height around it. A vector (SVG) version is still to do.
- **Colors:** Ink `#0B0B0B`, Paper `#FFFFFF`, Signal `#FF5A1F` (single accent, use sparingly), Mist `#8A8F98` (secondary text).
- **Type:** Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for code and numbers.
- **Voice:** short, honest, concrete. Show numbers and failures. No hype words.
- **X series title:** `Building Pigeon 🕊️ · Day N`
- **Post visual:** a screenshot, terminal GIF or short screen recording on Paper or Ink background with the logo bottom-right.
